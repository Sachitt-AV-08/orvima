Every AI tool wants a browser.

Almost all of them want it the wrong way.

The usual deal: hand your cookies to a cloud, pay per task, upload your live session to someone else's server, and trust nobody reads it. That is the standard price of "let the agent use the web."

I wanted the opposite, so I built Orvima.

Orvima gives any MCP-capable AI — Claude, Cursor, Copilot, your own agent — eyes and hands on your OWN Chrome or Edge. Your logins. Your machine. Your watch. Nothing leaves 127.0.0.1.

Three things I actually cared about:

→ Live viewport. You see exactly what the agent sees, streaming in real time. Not a spinner and a log line.

→ Human in the loop. Pause, resume, or approve any action before it commits.

→ Verified steps. Every action confirms the DOM result before the agent moves on. No silent failures, no guessed success.

Start with zero setup: `orvima demo` runs the entire agent loop against a built-in simulator — no browser, no keys, no account, nothing. Then install it with one line, point your MCP client at it, and it drives your real browser. No cloud. No API key. No signup.

And the part I wanted to prove: this post was composed and published by Orvima itself, driving my logged-in browser from the command line. One script, one terminal, a click sequence it made itself.

If Claude can see your screen, you already have this.
If Claude can use your accounts, that is a much bigger deal — and it should be your accounts, on your machine, where you can watch it happen.
